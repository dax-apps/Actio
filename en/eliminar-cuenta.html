Delete your Actio account and data

Last updated: September 21, 2026

This page explains how to request the deletion of your Actio account (developer: DAX-Apps) and its associated data. You can do it yourself from the app, or ask us by email.

Option 1: from the app (immediate)

  • Open Actio and sign in to your account.
  • Go to Settings → Account.
  • Tap “Delete account” and confirm in the prompt that appears.
  • The account and its data are deleted immediately, and you are signed out.

Option 2: by email

If you cannot open the app (for example, because you uninstalled it or lost access), write to us at dax.apps.mobile@gmail.com.

  • Send the email from the same address you signed up with (or state the email of your Google account).
  • Use “Delete Actio account” as the subject and include the email address of your account.
  • We will delete your account and its data as soon as possible and, at most, within 30 days, and we will confirm it by email.

Data that is deleted

When you delete your account, the following is permanently deleted and cannot be recovered:

  • Your account and access to it: email address, name linked to your Google account and sign-in credentials.
  • Your training data stored in the cloud: routines, exercises (with sets, reps, weight and RIR), calendar, exercise bank, tags and statistics.
  • The devices linked to your account (name/model and time zone) and the confirmation dates of your training streak.
  • Your subscription status in Actio.

Data that is kept and for how long

  • Free-trial device identifier: a technical identifier, without your name or email, kept while Actio is in operation, only so that the 7-day trial cannot be restarted by reinstalling the app or creating another account.
  • Rate-limit records (for example, for password resets): they only contain timestamps from the last 24 hours, to limit abuse.
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): kept for a maximum of 90 days in Firebase, and they do not include the content of your routines or your password.
  • Purchase data that Google Play keeps on its own (payment and billing history) is governed by Google Play’s policy, not by Actio.

Before deleting your account: subscription

Deleting the account does not cancel your Google Play subscription. If you have an active subscription and do not want to be charged any more, cancel it (before or after) from the subscription settings in Google Play.

Guest mode (no account)

If you use Actio without an account, there is no account to delete: your training data is only on your device and is erased when you uninstall the app. The only thing kept on the server is the device identifier described above.
