Unlike other workout apps, Actio doesn't tell you what to do; it gives you full control to organize your own training. Built for people who want to train and just need a tool that adapts to how they like to organize themselves.

Everything you need to stay organized

Routines your way

Create, reorder, duplicate and delete routines whenever you like. Independent sets with optional reps, weight and RIR, which you go through one by one in each exercise, plus notes and a configurable rest timer.

A calendar you control

Assign routines to the exact days you choose, with daily, weekly or monthly repetition if you need it.

Exercise bank

Save your favorite exercises in a reusable catalog and add them to any routine in seconds.

Complete statistics

Training streak, personal records, weight, volume and RIR history, estimated 1RM per exercise, most trained exercise, distribution by day of the week and your lifetime accumulated activity in the app.

Organize with tags

Classify and filter your routines and exercises however you prefer.

Your data, in the cloud

With your account, your routines and progress are saved in the cloud and synced across up to 2 devices. Actio needs an internet connection to work.

Try it without signing up

Guest mode gives you a 7-day trial with no sign-up and no payment method: your data is saved only on your device, and if you create an account later, you keep it.
