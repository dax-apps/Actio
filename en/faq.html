Do I need an internet connection to use Actio?

Yes. Actio needs a connection (mobile data or wifi) to open and check your account or trial, and to sync your data between devices and manage your subscription.

Is Actio a medical app?

No. Actio is not a medical or health-care app: it is a tool to organize your workouts. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any illness or injury, and it is no substitute for the advice of a doctor, physiotherapist or qualified trainer.

Can I use Actio without the help of a professional?

Actio is designed for people who already have some training knowledge and know which exercises they want to do. Always use it with good judgment and, if you need it (for example if you are just starting out, have an injury or a medical condition), with the help of a qualified professional. Talk to your doctor before starting any exercise program.

What is Actio designed for, and what must it not be used for?

For organizing routines, planning them on a calendar and tracking your progress. It must not be used for any other purpose: not as a medical or diagnostic tool, not to prescribe workouts or treatments to others, and not for anything else it is not designed for. The details are in the terms and conditions.

Can I try Actio without creating an account?

Yes. Guest mode gives you a 7-day trial with full access, with no sign-up and no payment method. During that time your data is saved only on your device; if you uninstall the app or change phones without creating an account, you will lose it. If you create an account during the trial, you keep everything you had already created and can access it from another mobile device too.

Can I restart the trial by uninstalling the app?

No. The trial is tied to the device, not to the installation or the account: reinstalling the app or creating another account does not restart the 7 days.

What statistics does Actio offer?

Training streak, personal records, weight, volume and RIR history per exercise, estimated 1RM, most trained exercise, distribution by day of the week and total accumulated activity.

What are RIR and estimated 1RM?

RIR (reps in reserve) is how many more reps you could have done at the end of a set; you can log it optionally. Estimated 1RM is the maximum weight you could lift for a single rep, calculated from your sets. It is only a rough estimate, not an exact measurement: do not use it to choose loads without judgment, or without a professional’s supervision if you need it.

Is it free?

All users get a 7-day free trial with full access, with or without an account. You do not need to sign up or add a payment method to try Actio. After the trial, continued access requires a monthly subscription through Google Play, from €1.50/month (excluding taxes). The final price (with taxes and your country’s currency included) is shown on Google Play before you subscribe, and may vary slightly by country.

How do I cancel my subscription?

From the subscription settings in Google Play, not from the app itself. The subscription renews automatically unless you cancel before the renewal date.

On how many devices can I use my account?

Up to 2 devices signed in at the same time. You can see and sign out any of them from Account → Devices.

Does Actio give me routines or training plans?

No. Actio does not provide guided routines or automatic plans — it is designed for people who already know which exercises they want to do and need a tool to organize them, plan them on a calendar and track their progress.

If I lose or change my phone, do I lose my data?

No, as long as you have an account and are signed in (in guest mode your data is only on your device): your routines, calendar and progress are also saved in the cloud, and you can access them from any other device by signing in with the same account.

Can I delete my account and my data?

Yes, at any time from Account → Delete account, inside the app. The account and all associated data are deleted. → Delete your Actio account and data

Which languages is Actio available in?

Español, galego, català, euskara, English, Deutsch, Français, Italiano and Português. You can choose the language in Settings or on the welcome screen the first time you open the app.
