Actio Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

This policy explains what data Actio collects, what it is used for and what rights you have over it.

1. Data controller

DAX-Apps, dax.apps.mobile@gmail.com.

2. What data we collect

Account data

  • Email address (when you sign up with email and password, or sign in with Google).
  • The name linked to your Google account, if you sign in with Google.
  • Your subscription status (free trial, active, expired) and purchase date, validated through Google Play.

Your training data

  • The routines, exercises (with their sets, reps, weight and RIR), calendar, tags and statistics you create inside the app. They are stored on your device and, if you are signed in, also on our servers (Firebase) so you can access them from another device.
  • The dates on which you complete all the routines of a day, which the server confirms in order to calculate your training streak.

Guest mode (no account)

  • If you try Actio without creating an account, your training data is stored only on your device and is not sent to our servers. The only thing sent is the device identifier described below, to record when your 7-day trial started. If you create an account later, that data is uploaded to it.

Device data

  • A device identifier (not linked to your personal identity, derived from the app’s Android identifier) to enforce the limit of 2 signed-in devices per account and to prevent abuse of the free trial. It is kept even if you uninstall the app or delete your account, precisely so that the trial cannot be restarted.
  • Integrity checks (Firebase App Check with Play Integrity), to verify that requests come from the genuine app on a real device. They are not used for advertising.
  • Device name/model, so you can identify it in your account’s "Devices" list.
  • Time zone, to calculate your training streak correctly.

Other data

  • If you request a password reset, we keep a record of those requests linked to your email, only to limit abuse.
  • If you write to us by email from the app, we receive your email address and the content of your message.

Crash reports

  • If the app crashes, a technical report (Firebase Crashlytics) is sent automatically with information about the error and the device, so we can fix it. It does not include the content of your routines or your password.

What we do NOT collect

  • We do not use advertising identifiers or show ads.
  • We do not share your data with third parties for advertising or commercial purposes.
  • We do not access your location, contacts, camera or microphone.

3. What we use this data for

  • Managing your account and session.
  • Syncing your routines and progress between your devices.
  • Validating your subscription and giving you access to paid features.
  • Enforcing the device limit and preventing abuse of the free trial and of password resets.
  • Detecting and fixing technical failures.

4. Who we share data with

We use Firebase (Google) as infrastructure for authentication, data storage and crash detection, and Google Play Billing to manage subscriptions. These providers process data on our behalf, under their own privacy policies and data processing agreements with Google. We do not sell or share your data with anyone else.

5. How long we keep your data

Your training and account data, for as long as your account exists. The free trial device identifier is kept longer, in order to prevent abuse. You can delete your account and all its associated data at any time from Account → Delete account, inside the app.

6. Your rights

If you live in the European Union, you have the right to access, rectify, erase and port your data, and to object to its processing. You can exercise these rights by deleting your account directly from the app, or by writing to us at dax.apps.mobile@gmail.com.

7. Minors

Actio is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from minors of that age.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app’s functionality changes. We will publish any relevant change on this same page.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy or about your data, write to us at dax.apps.mobile@gmail.com.
